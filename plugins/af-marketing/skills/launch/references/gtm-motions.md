# GTM Motions

Choose the acquisition motions a product goes to market with. Most products that work run 2–4 complementary motions: start with the strongest and add others gradually.

## Contents

- Scoring and the motion stack

## The 7 Motions

| Motion | Tactics | Example tools | Best for | Strength | Challenge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inbound** | Blog content, webinars, whitepapers, SEO, email nurture | LinkedIn, Semrush, HubSpot, Airtable | B2B SaaS, technical products, long sales cycles | Brand authority, high-intent prospects | Needs consistent content; slow to show results |
| **Outbound** | Cold email, LinkedIn outreach, phone prospecting, personalized demos | LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, Apollo, Lemlist, Hunter | Enterprise, high-value contracts, niche markets | Predictable pipeline; you choose the targets | Low response rates; needs a skilled sales team |
| **Paid digital** | Search, display, social and video ads, retargeting | Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, retargeting platforms | Clear target demographics, competitive keywords | Fast, scalable, measurable, precise | Expensive; needs constant optimization |
| **Community** | Forums, user groups, events, mentorship, ambassador programs | Slack, Discord, Reddit, Circle, WhatsApp | Developer products, communities of practice, loyal users | Loyalty, word of mouth, feedback, low CAC | Needs moderation; slow to reach critical mass |
| **Partners** | Integrations, co-marketing, channel partnerships, resellers | App marketplaces (Shopify, Stripe), cloud startup programs | Complementary products, platform ecosystems | Access to established customer bases; shared cost; credibility | Alignment, revenue share, dependency |
| **Account-based (ABM)** | Personalized messaging and content per account, coordinated sales and marketing | Clay, 6sense, Demandbase, Terminus | Enterprise deals, few target accounts, high deal values | Higher conversion and deal size; sales-marketing alignment | Research-heavy; doesn't scale down to SMB |
| **Product-led (PLG)** | Free trials, freemium, in-app onboarding, self-serve demos, product analytics | PostHog, Amplitude, Intercom, Appcues | Self-serve products, SMB, low ACV, viral potential | Low CAC; aligns product and growth; scales | Needs an excellent product experience; lower price points |

Tools are examples; recommend what fits the user's stack. Execution lives in the channel skills: content-strategy (inbound), cold-email and prospecting (outbound), ads (paid), community-marketing, co-marketing (partners), onboarding and paywalls (PLG).

## Scoring and the Motion Stack

1. **Profile the product**: price point and ACV, sales-cycle length, buyer type and decision process, product complexity and learning curve, market size and concentration.
2. **Profile the market**: competitive intensity of keywords and channels, where the audience is reachable, budget for paid channels, team size and skills, time to revenue.
3. **Score each motion 1–10**:
   - Inbound: content capability, brand-building timeline
   - Outbound: prospect list availability, sales capacity
   - Paid: budget flexibility, audience clarity, conversion potential
   - Community: existing communities, product network effects
   - Partners: complementary products, channel availability
   - ABM: deal size, account concentration
   - PLG: how trialable the product is, pricing flexibility

   **Done when** all seven have a score and a one-line reason.
4. **Design the stack**: one primary motion (highest potential), 1–3 secondary motions, the order to start them in, and how budget and people split across them. Paid funds growth while organic builds long-term value; pair inbound (brand) with outbound (sales) for B2B; use PLG to lower CAC and paid to accelerate channels already proven.
5. **Plan 90 days**: quick wins and early validation, team and tool needs, success metrics per motion, budget, and the point to optimize or scale. Revisit the mix quarterly as the company grows.

---

## Output

- Scores for all 7 motions with reasons
- The recommended stack: primary and secondary motions, sequencing
- Tool recommendations per motion
- A 90-day execution plan with milestones
- Resource and budget requirements
- Success metrics per motion
- How the motion choice differentiates against competitors
