# /battlecard

Create a one-page battlecard that helps reps win deals against a specific competitor.

## Usage

```
/battlecard $ARGUMENTS
```

Examples:

```
/battlecard Our CRM vs Salesforce
/battlecard ProjectFlow vs Monday.com for mid-market teams
/battlecard [upload competitor materials or win/loss data]
```

## Workflow

1. **Identify the matchup.** Read `company.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md`, then ask, in one message, for whatever they leave open: the competitor, the typical buyer choosing between you, the deal stage where they meet (early evaluation, final decision, displacement), and any win/loss data or sales feedback. Wait for the answers.
2. **Build the card** with the Battle Cards section of the **sales-enablement** skill: its research steps, then its structure. Stop when every section is filled or marked unknown, and the card is saved as markdown.
3. **Offer next steps:**
   - Battlecards for other competitors
   - A full competitor profile with the **competitor-profiling** skill
   - Customer-facing comparison pages with the **competitors** skill
   - Updated positioning with the **product-marketing** skill

Position on your strengths rather than their weaknesses, refresh the card quarterly, and keep it to one page: reps read it mid-call.
