# Business Model Canvas

Nine building blocks showing how value flows through the organization. Fill them in this order: segments, value propositions, relationships and channels, activities and resources, partners, costs, revenue.

## Blocks

**Creating value (left side)**

1. **Key partners**: strategic partners and suppliers, the partnerships the model depends on, the activities partners handle, and joint-venture or co-creation opportunities.
2. **Key activities**: the processes critical to delivering value (production, problem-solving, platform or network), and which are in-house or outsourced.
3. **Key resources**: physical, intellectual, human and financial resources that enable the activities, and the minimum viable set.

**The value proposition (centre)**

4. **Value propositions**: the value delivered per segment: problems solved, needs met, the products and services behind them; quantitative (price, speed, quality) and qualitative (design, status).

**Delivering value (right side)**

5. **Customer relationships**: personal assistance, self-service, automated, community or co-creation; acquisition and retention cost; how customers stay engaged.
6. **Channels**: awareness, purchase, delivery and after-sales; direct or indirect, owned or partner.
7. **Customer segments**: mass market, niche, segmented or multi-sided platform; each segment's defining needs, channels, relationships and profitability.

**Financial viability (bottom)**

8. **Cost structure**: the most important costs, fixed vs variable, cost drivers (scale, automation, labour, infrastructure), and whether the business is cost-driven or value-driven.
9. **Revenue streams**: per customer, per transaction, subscription, licensing or rent; pricing mechanism (fixed, dynamic, value-based); lifetime value and unit economics.

Strong models have a clear, defensible value proposition that every other block reinforces. Use the canvas to find opportunities for innovation and optimization.
