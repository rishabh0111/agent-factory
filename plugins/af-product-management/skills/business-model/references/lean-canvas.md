# Lean Canvas

Built for rapid hypothesis testing: go fast, attack the riskiest assumptions first, and update as you learn. Useful to align a founding team.

## Blocks

**Product definition**

1. **Problem**: the top 3 customer problems or needs, their pains and frustrations, and the unsatisfactory solutions they use now.
2. **Solution**: the top 3 features or approaches, how each addresses a problem, and why it is novel or better.
3. **Unique value proposition**: one concise, memorable statement of why customers choose you over alternatives: what makes you different, not just better.
4. **Unfair advantage**: what competitors can't easily replicate: network effects, brand, IP, switching costs.

**Market and traction**

5. **Customer segments**: the target customer, early adopters and the first segment, personas, and the size of the addressable market. Target 1-2 segments.
6. **Channels**: primary acquisition channels, distribution and sales approach, how customers find you.
7. **Revenue streams**: pricing model or revenue per customer, lifetime value (LTV), revenue growth assumptions.

**Economics and validation**

8. **Cost structure**: fixed costs (salaries, infrastructure, facilities), variable costs (COGS, transaction costs, support), key cost drivers, customer acquisition cost (CAC).
9. **Key metrics**: 3-5 critical metrics: activation (how users get value quickly), retention (how many stay), revenue, and the North Star metric.
