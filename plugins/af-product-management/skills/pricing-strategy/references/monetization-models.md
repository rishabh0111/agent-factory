# Monetization models

| Model | How it works | Best fit | Risks | Validation experiment | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Flat-rate** | One price for the product | Simple products with predictable costs | Leaves money on the table with heavy users | Test one price on a landing page | Basecamp |
| **Per-seat** | Price per user, team or department | B2B collaboration tools adopted by teams | Sales complexity, contract length, implementation overhead | 5-10 customer interviews validating a per-seat price and support model | Slack, Figma |
| **Usage-based** | Pay per volume: API calls, storage, transactions | Infrastructure, APIs, B2B platforms with variable needs | Unpredictable revenue, customer cost anxiety, customers optimizing usage down | Track usage, pilot with 5-10 beta customers, model revenue | AWS, Twilio |
| **Tiered** | Free / Pro / Enterprise or similar, each for a segment | Products with distinct segments and willingness to pay | Muddy tier boundaries, feature-gating fights | A/B test tier names, bundles and anchors | Most SaaS |
| **Freemium** | Free core, paid advanced features | High-volume, low-touch products with viral or network effects | Low conversion (typically 1-5%); the upgrade value must be obvious | Launch a free tier, track conversion, collect upgrade feedback | Spotify, Notion |
| **Freemium + usage** | Free entry, then metered | Platform products | Bill shock, free-tier abuse | Meter a beta cohort and model costs | Vercel, OpenAI API |
| **Subscription** | Recurring monthly or annual charge for ongoing access | Products with continuous value | Churn; annual vs monthly cannibalization | Offer a subscription to beta customers, measure churn and NPS | |
| **Value-based** | Price set on the customer outcome delivered | High-impact enterprise tools | Hard to quantify value; long sales cycles | Founder-led sales conversations testing willingness to pay | Salesforce, Palantir |
| **One-time purchase** | Single upfront purchase or licence | Niche tools and templates, not ongoing services | Revenue concentrated at launch; no recurring revenue; update and support questions | Limited offering; track conversion and satisfaction | |
| **Marketplace / transaction fee** | Percentage or fixed fee per transaction between buyers and sellers | Platforms connecting supply and demand | Chicken-and-egg liquidity, trust and safety, fee pressure | MVP with a few sellers and a free period to seed supply; model unit economics | |
| **Advertising / sponsorship** | Ads, sponsored content, brand partnerships | High-traffic consumer products | Brand damage, worse UX, advertiser concentration | Ads to a small user segment; measure engagement and revenue impact | |
| **Hybrid** | A combination of the above | Complex products with several value levers | Complexity for the customer | Test the combination with one segment first | |
