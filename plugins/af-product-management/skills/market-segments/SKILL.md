# Market Segments

Identify 3-5 distinct customer segments across the addressable market, each with its job to be done, pains, gains and product fit, to choose target audiences or a market to expand into. This looks outward at the market, including people who are not customers yet; to group *existing* users by behavior from usage or feedback data, use the `user-segmentation` skill.

Read `company.md` first for the product, current customers and positioning; ask only for what is missing. Read any research, market studies, customer databases or existing segmentation documents the user provides.

## Steps

1. **Explore the market**: the full addressable market for the product. The `market-sizing` skill sizes it in depth.
2. **Choose segmentation dimensions**: behavioral, demographic, firmographic or needs-based, whichever separate buyers with different jobs. Done when the chosen dimensions are named with a reason.
3. **Define 3-5 segments** that are measurable, reachable and distinct. Done when no customer would plausibly sit in two.
4. **Characterize each segment** with the fields below, grounded in available data. Done when every field is filled or marked *needs research*.
5. **Assess the opportunity** per segment: size, growth, competitive intensity, fit. Prioritize segments with clear jobs and sharp pains; consider both greenfield and underserved segments. Done when the segments are ranked with a recommended target.

## Per segment

- **Name and overview**: a memorable name; size estimate (% of market or absolute numbers when data exists); growth trajectory.
- **Demographics or firmographics**: age, role, company size, industry, geography; decision-maker profile if B2B.
- **Job to be done**: primary job and desired outcome; its frequency, context and stakes; success criteria.
- **Pains**: barriers to getting the job done; consequences of leaving it unsolved.
- **Gains**: outcomes that matter most; preferred solution traits; cost and time constraints.
- **Product fit**: how well the product serves the job, the value proposition for this segment, and adoption barriers.
- **Competitive landscape**: existing solutions, workarounds and alternatives this segment uses.

Flag every segment assumption that needs more market research. Save as markdown.
