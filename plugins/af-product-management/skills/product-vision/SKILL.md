# Product Vision

Draft a product vision that is **inspiring** (it makes people want to commit), **achievable** (credible given resources, market and capabilities) and **emotional** (it creates meaning, not just direction). A vision answers: how do we inspire people, what are we aspiring to achieve, and what values do we uphold? It is a living statement that evolves with the strategy.

Read `company.md` first for the product, customers, mission and values; ask only for what is missing, such as the current state and market position. Read any files the user points to.

## Steps

1. **Core problem.** Name the problem the product exists to solve and for whom. Done when it fits in one sentence.
2. **Future state.** Describe the ideal future for customers and for the company if the product succeeds. Done when the picture covers both.
3. **Options.** Draft 3-5 distinct vision statements, each memorable and sayable in one sentence, in plain, emotionally resonant language without jargon. Done when the options differ in angle, not just wording.
4. **Choose.** Pick the strongest and explain why, from the view of customers, employees and investors, and how it fits the company's values and market opportunity. Done when the choice has a reason from each of the three views.

Balance ambition with credibility: a vision nobody believes motivates nobody. To build the rest of the strategy around it, use the `product-strategy` skill.

## Output

```
## Product Vision: [Product]

### Recommended vision
"[One sentence]"
Why: [customers / employees / investors], fit with values and opportunity

### Alternatives considered
1. "[...]": [angle]
```
