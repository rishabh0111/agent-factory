# Company

Every specialist and department skill reads this file: `/af-setup` imports it into the project `CLAUDE.md`. Fill each section once and update it as the company changes. Keep each section short, specific and current.

## Products

What you sell, to whom, at what price, and what makes each product different. One block per product.

## Customers

Who buys: segments, the ideal customer, the people involved in a purchase, and the problems they hire you to solve.

## Voice

How the company sounds: tone, words you use and words you avoid, and two or three short examples of writing that sounds right.

## Policies

Rules the company works by: refunds, discounts, pricing authority, support hours, data handling, legal positions, approvals, benefits and leave, compliance frameworks, fiscal year.

## Limits

What specialists hand to a person before acting, beyond their defaults: spend thresholds, contract values, public statements, hiring decisions, regulated topics.

## Systems

Where things live: CRM, ticketing, accounting, HR system, data warehouse, docs. Name each tool and what it is the source of truth for.

## Glossary

Terms with a specific meaning here: product names, internal acronyms, customer tiers, metric definitions.

## Learnings

Dated one-line entries for things that went wrong or worked unexpectedly well, so the next task starts from them.
